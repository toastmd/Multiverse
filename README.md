# MULTIVERSE MUD: a browser MUD in the LPMud tradition (fantasy, sci-fi and the Old West)

Requires Node 18+ (`brew install node`). No npm packages.

    cd ashfall
    node server.js              # then open http://localhost:4000
    PORT=8080 HOST=127.0.0.1 node server.js

Other devices on your network can join at http://<your-mac-ip>:4000 (System Settings > Network shows the IP).
The first account created becomes a wizard. Accounts are saved in data/players/.

## Layout (driver / mudlib split)
- driver.js    objects, move(), heart_beat scheduler, call_out, reset() timer
- quests.js    NPCs and quests (hot-reloadable)
- quest.js     quest engine and the gossip channel
- areas.js     levels 8-40: gear tiers, potions, shops, temple, new areas and generated monsters
- classes.js   classes, spells, recipes (hot-reloadable)
- magic.js     spell engine: damage, heals, buffs, summons, gathering, crafting
- lib.js       Room, Item, Living, Player, Monster, combat, map data (mudlib)
- world.js     rooms, monsters, items. Edit while running, then type `update` as a wizard
- commands.js  verb parser. Items can carry their own verbs (see the fountain's `drink`)
- server.js    HTTP + WebSocket, accounts (scrypt), autosave every 60s
- public/index.html  client: HP/XP bars, live map, animated combat arena

## MULTIVERSE MUD rules (original; no third-party game content)
The setting mixes three genres: Old West towns with saloons (Ashfall Junction, Redrock Trail, Silverado Mines), fantasy realms (Whisperwood, Barrow Crypts, Sunken Marsh, Sunscorch Dunes, Shattered Citadel) and sci-fi worlds (Frostpeak Station, Ember Wastes, the Abyss). The mechanics are generic ideas, with original names and numbers:
- **Dice pools:** attacks and defense roll pools of d6; a 5 or 6 is a hit. Net hits add damage, six or more is a critical, more than half ones with no hits is a fumble. Targets resist spells with a defense pool. `score` shows your pools and attributes (Brawn, Reflex, Grit, Mind, Will, Charm).
- **Luck:** a small pool of points. One automatically halves a killing blow, or `push` adds six attack dice. Luck refills on level-up, at the clinics and at the inn.
- **Cyberware and Aura:** the Chrome Clinic (west of the inn) sells chrome (`list`, `install`, `uninstall`, `cyber`). Every piece costs Aura out of 6.0, and less Aura means a smaller mana pool. Chromeblades love it, Arcanists beware.
- **Credits** replace gold. Quests are contracts; quest points are shown as before. Classes were renamed for the setting, and the Wirewalker (Net hacker) was added.
- **Sounds:** level-up, contract complete and death jingles play in the browser (toggle in the sidebar). They are original, synthesized for this project and dedicated to the public domain (CC0), see public/sounds/LICENSE.txt.

## Characters, display options and announcements
- **Gender:** choose male, female or nonbinary when you create a character (`gender` shows or changes it). It appears on `who` and in your portrait.
- **Display options:** the gear button in the browser sidebar changes the text font and size, and turns on keypad controls (Num Lock on: 8 north, 2 south, 4 west, 6 east, 9 up, 3 down, 5 look, 0 pack, 1 flee, 7 score, + spells, - contracts, / who; only when the command line is empty). The vitals bars are large, with a big HP bar.
- **Mud-wide announcements:** every level gained, every contract completed, and every death (a random NPC taunts the player by name) is announced to everyone online.
- **Portrait window:** under the combat view. It shows the picture of the NPC you talk to or the monster you fight; with no picture it shows a generated portrait of you (class and gender). 16 NPC portraits are in public/img/npc (generated by tools/make_npc_images.js; run it again to regenerate). Drop a file named mob_<monster id>.svg there to give a monster its own picture, or <npc id>.svg for an NPC.
- **Resident bots:** Zeph (Wirewalker) and Rook (Chromeblade) log in at startup, appear on `who` and the map, hunt, shop, heal, gossip and level like players. They avoid areas where they die. Their state is saved in data/bots.json. Names Zeph and Rook cannot be used for accounts. Edit bots.js to change or add bots.

## Themes, sound, YouTube and bounties
- **Themes and volume:** settings has seven browser themes (black, white, dark grey, dark blue, Old West, futuristic, fantasy) and a volume slider; both are remembered in the browser. New sound effects (original, made by `tools/make_sounds.py`): login, legendary find (everyone online hears it) and tradeskill level-up.
- **YouTube in gossip:** paste a youtube.com or youtu.be link in gossip and everyone sees a thumbnail that plays in place (youtube-nocookie embed, one video per player every 10 seconds). Players can turn videos off in settings.
- **Bounties:** every so often (up to three at a time, 40 minutes each) a quest giver marks a monster WANTED and announces it. The first player or team to kill it collects credits at once plus bounty points. `bounties` lists them, and bounty points count x40 toward the leaderboard score. Defined in `bounty.js`.
- **Guide:** area sections, the bestiary, shops and NPC lists are styled by genre (Old West, fantasy, sci-fi).
- **Checks:** `node tools/check_world.js` confirms every room is reachable and every exit has a way back.
- **Pixabay key:** `pixabay.apiKey` in `data/config.json` is used by `tools/fetch_pixabay.py`.

## Teams, feelings, triggers, gag, tutorial
- **Teams:** `team invite <player>`, `team accept`, `team leave`, `team kick`, `team lead`, `tc <message>`. A team of up to 5 splits the XP and credits of every kill among members in the same room, with a 10% bonus per extra member; teammates in the room also get kill credit for contracts.
- **Feelings:** `socials` lists about 50 (smile, cheer, dance, wave, bow, laugh, hug...). Use one alone or aimed at someone (`smile bob`). Definitions are in `socials.js`.
- **Triggers:** in settings, or `trigger add <text> => <command>`. They run in the browser when a line of game text matches (plain text or /regex/, `$1` for a captured group), can highlight lines, and are saved with your character. Chat lines never fire triggers, and a cooldown stops loops.
- **Gag:** `gag on` hides every miss and fumble in combat; `gag off` shows them again.
- **Newbie tutorial:** a dialog tour with clickable action buttons and a progress checklist. It opens for new characters, has an Exit tutorial button, and comes back with `tutorial`. `travel <place>` walks to landmarks along safe rooms.
- **Bots and taunts:** Zeph and Rook now have over forty random gossip lines and answer players on gossip (greetings, help, teams, bosses, loot and more). Death taunts have several lines per NPC plus a general pool.

## Posting announcements to X/Twitter
Set `TWITTER_API_KEY`, `TWITTER_API_SECRET`, `TWITTER_ACCESS_TOKEN` and `TWITTER_ACCESS_SECRET` (or `twitter` in `data/config.json`) from an app with read and write access at developer.x.com. Legendary finds, boss kills, remorts and every 10th level are posted with `#game #Multiverse` plus a tag for the event type (`TWITTER_EVENTS` and `TWITTER_HASHTAGS` change this, `TWITTER_DRY_RUN=1` only logs). Posts are at least 60 seconds apart. Wizards can use `twitter` for status and `twitter test`.

## The five newer areas
Tombstone Gulch (Old West ghost town, levels 10-14, from the Redrock Trail), Thornwild Court (fantasy fey realm, 15-19, from the Sunken Marsh), Starship Aurora (sci-fi crash site, 22-26, from the Sunscorch Dunes), The Drowned Reach (fantasy sunken kingdom, 27-31, from the frozen waterfall) and Brimstone Rail Yard (Old West hell depot, 32-36, from the Ember Wastes). Each has about 14 rooms, four kinds of monsters, a boss with a legendary item, a shop, a safe camp, an NPC with three contracts and treasure drops that sell for credits. Defined in `newareas.js`, `quests2.js` and `legends.js`.

## Legendary items and the leaderboard
Every boss (fourteen now) carries one unique legendary item (25% drop chance): Gravewarden's Oathblade, Heart of the Nine-Headed Mother, Red Jack's Golden Peacemaker, The Motherlode Nugget, Pharaoh's Sunheart Aegis, Cryo-Core Bulwark, Heart of the Furnace Star, Crown of the Dread Marshal and The Multiverse Key. Equipment versions carry bonus stats, treasures are simply priceless, and all of them sell for their FULL value (normal gear sells for 40%). Whoever finds one is announced to the whole MUD. Definitions are in `legends.js`.
The achievements leaderboard (`leaderboard`, the top 10 button, or the L key) is a draggable window ranking players by level (a remort counts as 40 levels) x100, quests completed x250 and tradeskill levels raised x40.

## Pictures
All pictures are generated by the renderer in `tools/render/` (Python with numpy, scipy and Pillow; nothing is downloaded, no third-party art). Run `python3 tools/render_art.py` (or `terrain`, `portraits`, `scenes`, `icons`) to regenerate them; the results are already included, so you do not need Python to run the game.
- `public/img/terrain/` tileable terrain textures used by the map (grass, dirt, stone, concrete, metal)
- `public/img/player/<class>-<m|f|n>.jpg` portraits for every class and gender (also shown by `who`)
- `public/img/npc/<id>.jpg` portraits for the 16 NPCs
- `public/guide/img/` banners, area landscapes and icons for the Player's Guide (rebuild the guide with `node tools/build_guide.js`)
These are lit, shaded procedural paintings (soft 3D lighting, bokeh, neon rim light), not photographs.

**Real Old West photos from Pixabay:** get a free API key at pixabay.com/api/docs, then run `PIXABAY_API_KEY=yourkey python3 tools/fetch_pixabay.py photos` (sets: `west` by default, or `landscapes`, `terrain`, `icons`, `portraits`, `all`; `--list` shows the searches). It uses Pixabay's official API (answers cached 24 hours, one best match per picture, files downloaded rather than hotlinked) and writes `CREDITS.txt` and a `contact_sheet.html` so you can review the picks. Delete any you dislike, then run `python3 tools/import_art.py photos`. Pixabay photos are free to use under its Content License, but read it before using photos of identifiable people (the portraits set is opt-in for that reason).

**Using real, photographic images instead:** the built-in art cannot be photorealistic. `ART_PROMPTS.md` has a text-to-image prompt for all 126 pictures. Generate them with any image tool, name each file after its key (for example `player-magician-f.png`, `npc-torv.jpg`, `area-town.webp`), put them in one folder and run `python3 tools/import_art.py <folder>`. It crops, resizes and installs each one (any you skip keep the built-in art). Add `--dry` to preview.

## Player conveniences
`autoflee on|off` flees automatically at 30% health. `alias g get all` makes your own shortcuts (several commands with `;`; `alias` lists, `unalias` removes). `keypad` (or the Edit keypad keys panel in settings) re-maps the numeric keypad; aliases, keypad layout and autoflee are saved with your character. `who` opens a window with portraits of everyone online. The gossip channel is at the bottom of the left column. `drink fountain` in Ashfall Square restores 100% HP and mana. Contract XP grows with contract level.

## GIPHY GIFs in gossip
Players type `gossip ;hi` (or `/g ;hi`, or `gossip good game ;applause`): the words after the semicolon are searched on GIPHY and a matching GIF appears in everyone's gossip window (and its link goes to the Discord bridge). Get a free API key at developers.giphy.com and set `GIPHY_API_KEY` (or `giphy.apiKey` in `data/config.json`). `GIPHY_RATING` (g, pg, pg-13, r; default pg) controls content, `GIPHY_COOLDOWN` (default 6 seconds per player) protects your API quota. Results are cached for 10 minutes, only GIPHY's own media addresses are ever shown, and players can hide GIFs in settings. A semicolon inside an alias separates commands, so put GIF gossip in an alias carefully.

## Login notices, the Discord bridge and GitHub sync
Settings come from environment variables or `data/config.json` (copy `config.example.json`). `data/config.json` is never synced or committed. Tokens in environment variables also work with launchd (`EnvironmentVariables` in the plist).

**Login and logout notices:** when a player logs in or out, everyone online sees "Name (Remort 2, L12 Dragon) has logged in." (not the bots, and not for a mere reconnect). Each player can mute them with `notify off`.

**Discord bridge (gossip):**
- *MUD to Discord:* in Discord, Channel Settings, Integrations, Webhooks, New Webhook, copy the URL into `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL`. Every gossip line (including boss kills) is posted to the channel under the player's name, with all @mentions neutralised. Optional: `DISCORD_ANNOUNCEMENTS=1` also posts level-ups, contracts and death taunts; `DISCORD_PRESENCE=1` also posts logins and logouts.
- *Discord to MUD:* create a bot in the Discord developer portal, enable the **Message Content intent**, invite it to the channel with View Channel and Read Message History, and set `DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN` and `DISCORD_CHANNEL_ID`. Human messages appear in gossip as "[Discord] Name: text". Bot and webhook messages are ignored, so nothing echoes back. `DISCORD_POLL_SECONDS` (default 4) sets how often the channel is checked.
- Wizards can type `discord` for status and `discord test` to send a test message.

**GitHub sync:** set `GITHUB_TOKEN` (a fine-grained personal access token with **Contents: read and write** on just that repository) and `GITHUB_REPO=owner/name` (`GITHUB_BRANCH`, default main). The MUD then keeps the repository in step with its code and content using the GitHub REST API (no git needed): each sync is one commit containing every added, changed or deleted file. Triggers: area builder saves and deletes (pushed after `GITHUB_DEBOUNCE_SECONDS`, default 20, so rapid edits become one commit), a check for edited files every `GITHUB_SYNC_MINUTES` (default 5), the wizard command `github sync`, and `node tools/github_sync.js`. An empty repository is initialised automatically. Uploaded: source, `public/` (client, guide, art, sounds), `tools/`, and `data/custom/` (areas made in the builder). Never uploaded: player accounts, bot saves, the builder key, `data/config.json` and the sync state. Wizards can type `github` for status.

## Bound tools and the Bag of Holding
Every character automatically has seven **bound tools** (fishing pole, mining pick, herbalist's sickle, smithing hammer, portable alembic, cast-iron skillet, salvage crowbar) and a **Bag of Holding**. They load for everyone, cannot be dropped or sold, and are saved in the player file on logout. Tools improve from Mk I to Mk V with use (0 / 25 / 100 / 250 / 600 uses): faster gathering, extra finds, better rare finds, material-saving smithing, double batches when brewing and cooking, and bigger salvage hauls, plus bonus skill XP (`tools`). The bag holds 60 items plus 20 for every remort (860 at Remort 40). Gathered materials and looted gear go in automatically; crafting, salvaging and quest turn-ins use bag contents; `sell all` and `sell materials` sell them (`bag`, `bag put`, `bag get`, `bag sell`, `bag auto loot|overflow|off`).

## Player Guide website
Open **/guide/** on your server (for example http://localhost:4000/guide/). 21 pages grouped by type: getting started (how to play, interface, commands, FAQ), characters (classes, remorting, experience, attributes and combat, cyberware and implants), magic and skills (spells, tradeskills, tools and bag), items (materials, weapons and armor, potions and meals, shops), and the world (a full textured map with surface and underground layers, areas, bestiary, NPCs and quests). It is generated from the live game data, with procedurally generated art: banners, area landscapes, class portraits, icons and terrain textures. After changing classes, items, areas or quests, run `node tools/build_guide.js` to refresh it. The game's sidebar links to it.

## Travel, terrain and the map
- **recall:** type `recall` anywhere (not while fighting) to return to the starting square in Ashfall.
- **Terrain:** every room has a terrain: grass, dirt, stone, concrete or metal. The area sets the default (Ashfall concrete, the forest grass, the crypt stone, the marsh dirt, the mines stone, the abyss metal and so on) with per-room overrides: forges and clinics are metal, gardens and oases are grass, paths are dirt. `look` shows "Ground: ...". Builder areas have a default terrain and a terrain per room.
- **Map:** Ashfall (the first area) is fully mapped for every player from the start, other areas fill in as you explore. Rooms are drawn with texture images for their terrain (grass blades, dirt, stone slabs, concrete, riveted metal), paths take the texture of the room they leave, and your room has a glowing frame, a YOU label and your class icon. The legend sits under the map.

## Tradeskills
`tradeskills` lists every tradeskill and whether you know it; `tradeskills <name>` shows details and recipes. `learn <skill>` teaches it at any time, free, anywhere (`unlearn` drops it). Skills level 1 to 50 by use; Prospectors learn 50% faster and find extra.
- **Foraging** (`forage`), **Mining** (`mine`), **Fishing** (`fish`): gather herbs and blooms, ore, silver and mithril, fish and pearls from rooms with those resources. Rarer finds unlock at higher skill.
- **Smithing** (`smith <recipe>`, needs a forge): tools and every tier of weapon and armor, from scrap to dragonscale.
- **Brewing** (`brew <recipe>`): stims, trauma kits and mana cells.
- **Cooking** (`cook <recipe>`, needs a hearth: inn, camp, forge or safe room): meals that heal and give a timed buff (`eat` or `quaff` to use them).
- **Salvaging** (`salvage <item>`): break unworn gear into ore, silver, mithril and scale.
All gathered materials sell at every shop (`sell materials`, at least 1 credit each). The new **Salvage Exchange** (east of the Ironbound Armory) pays 60% instead of 40% and sells the basic materials.

## Quest window and score
- **Quest window:** the Quests button in the browser sidebar (or the Q key) opens a draggable window that lists only the quests you have accepted: who gave it and where, the quest text, each requirement with its progress bar (kill counts, items to bring, places to visit), a "ready to turn in" badge with where to hand it in, and the reward. It remembers its position, and opens by itself the first time you accept a quest. In the text client, `quests` shows the same information.
- **score:** shows name, remort, gender and class, level, experience and exactly how many XP you still need for the next level, all vitals (HP, mana or stamina, Luck, Aura), wealth in credits plus the sale value of your goods, materials (ore, herbs, fish and so on, counted by name), consumables, attributes, combat pools and weapon, chrome, and contract progress.

## Remort (levels 1 to 40, again, up to Remort 40)
At level 40, go to the **Hall of Rebirth** (west of the Temple of the Dawn) and type `remort`. `remort confirm <class>` resets you to level 1 as one of three remort classes, which cannot be picked at character creation: **Dragon** (breath weapons and scales), **Android** (precision weapons and nanite repair), **Shapeshifter** (claws, packs, regeneration and mist). Each has 10 abilities, unlocked at levels 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 22, 30 and 40, and spell power 40% above normal classes.
You become Remort 1, then Remort 2 and so on up to Remort 40, and `who` shows it ("Remort 3, L12 Dragon"). Each remort gives +4% max health and mana, +3% spell power, and +1 attack and defense die every 4 remorts, but levelling is 10% slower. You keep your credits, items (unequipped; wear them again as you level), chrome and implants, contracts and quest points, name and gender. Everyone online sees the announcement. Bots remort too.

## Neural implants (expensive stat boosts)
The **Neural Implant Lab** (west of the Chrome Clinic) sells a series of implants for six attributes: Brawn, Reflex, Grit, Mind, Will and Charm, each in five Marks. Mk I to V adds +1 to +5 to that attribute and costs 2,000 / 8,000 / 30,000 / 100,000 / 300,000 credits (level 1 / 12 / 24 / 36 / 40) and a little Aura each. They must be installed in order, and each upgrade trades the previous Mark in for half its price. Brawn, Reflex and Grit raise damage, dice pools and health; Mind adds mana; Will adds Luck; Charm increases loot credits. The older Back-Alley chrome (armor, pools, damage) is separate. `list`, `install <full name>` (e.g. `install brawn implant mk i`), `uninstall`, `cyber`.

## Area builder (grid GUI)
Open http://localhost:4000/builder.html. The builder key is printed in the server log at startup (also saved in data/builder.key; set BUILDER_KEY to choose your own). Anyone with the key can edit the world, so keep it private on a shared network.
Paint rooms on the grid, link them (auto-link or the link tool), set description, flags, monsters, items, shops, gathering nodes, up/down links and external links to existing rooms, create new monsters, then Save: the area goes live immediately (no restart) and is stored as data/custom/<area>.json. Load, edit, export/import or delete areas from the same page.

## Classes (pick one when you create a character)
Arcanist, Rogue, Alchemist, Gunslinger, Chromeblade, Mechwright, Necromancer, Preacher, Druid, Showman, Wirewalker: ten abilities each, unlocked at levels 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 22, 30 and 40.
Prospector: 13 skill-spells in four schools (Botany, Mining, Fishing, Crafting) with no combat spells. Gather at nodes (Old Quarry for ore,
Reed Pond for fish, forest rooms for herbs), then `craft` (forge recipes need Brann's Smithy). Prospectors carry 30 items instead of 15.
Commands: spells, cast <spell> [target] (or just type the spell name), class, craft. Edit classes.js and `update` to rebalance live.

## World (levels 1-40, gold, shops)
Ashfall has the Dawn Clinic (the Temple of the Dawn) (`pray`: free full heal and mana up to level 15, then a small donation), Brann's Smithy (free starter rack plus weapons),
the Ironbound Armory, and Market Row (potions and mana vials). Commands: list, buy [n] <item>, sell <item|all>, value <item>.
Areas by level: Whisperwood 1-5, Crypt 4-8, Sunken Marsh 8-13, Redrock Trail 12-16, Silverado Mines 12-17, Sunscorch Dunes 18-24 (Dune Bazaar),
Frostpeak 24-30 (Frostpeak Lodge), Ember Wastes 30-34, Shattered Citadel 35-38, The Abyss 39-40 (the Voidlord). 171 rooms with long descriptions (each area has 10 extra rooms, including one safe camp with faster regeneration), 41 monsters.
Gear comes in seven tiers with level requirements (iron to voidforged). Monster stats are generated from level in areas.js.

## Contracts (quests) and gossip
16 NPCs give 41 quests: kill, collect, visit and talk objectives, with chains and rewards. Start at the Adventurers' Guild (`board`), then `talk <npc>`,
`accept <quest>`, `quests`, `abandon`. Turn in by talking to the giver again. Each quest awards quest points (QP) once, on first completion; your total shows in `score`, `quests` and the browser sidebar. Progress shows live in the browser's quest tracker. NPCs and quests are in quests.js.
The gossip channel is global: `gossip <msg>` (or `/g msg` in the browser), `gossip off|on|history`. Boss kills and every fifth level are announced on it.

Wizard commands: update, goto <room>, clone <item>.

      <key>Label</key><string>com.ashfall.mud</string>
      <key>ProgramArguments</key><array><string>/opt/homebrew/bin/node</string><string>/Users/YOU/ashfall/server.js</string></array>
      <key>WorkingDirectory</key><string>/Users/YOU/ashfall</string>
      <key>RunAtLoad</key><true/><key>KeepAlive</key><true/>
      <key>StandardOutPath</key><string>/tmp/ashfall.log</string><key>StandardErrorPath</key><string>/tmp/ashfall.log</string>
    </dict></plist>

    launchctl load ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.ashfall.mud.plist
