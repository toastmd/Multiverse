Getting Started

How to Play

MULTIVERSE MUD is a text world you play in your browser. Here is everything you need for your first ten minutes.

Welcome to the junction. MULTIVERSE MUD mixes fantasy realms, sci-fi worlds and Old West towns with saloons. Every road starts in Ashfall Junction, where portals from every world meet.

1

Create your character

Pick a name (3 to 12 letters), a password, a class and a gender (male, female or other). Your class decides your spells and your strengths. It can only change by remorting at level 40. On a brand-new server the first account created becomes the wizard (administrator).

2

Look around

You begin in Ashfall Square, which is safe: nobody can fight here. Type look (or l) to see the room. The map in the right column already shows all of Ashfall, with a glowing YOU marker on your room. Move with n s e w u d, the compass buttons, or the keypad.

3

Heal, gear up, and stock up

North of the inn, the Dawn Clinic (Temple of the Dawn) heals you fully for free up to level 15: type pray. At Brann's Smithy (south from the square) a free rack holds starter gear: get all, then wear and wield what you find. Market Row sells red stims and mana cells (list, buy 2 potion).

4

Take your first contract

Visit the Adventurers' Guild and type board to see open contracts. talk torv then accept first starts First Steps. The quest window (button or the Q key) lists every accepted contract with exactly what it needs. Turn a finished contract in by talking to whoever gave it.

5

Hunt your first monsters

Leave through the North Gate to the Whisperwood. Rats and goblins are good first targets: kill rat. Cast spells by typing the spell name (spark) or cast spark; see spells for your list. Fights run automatically every two seconds. Watch your big HP bar: below half, drink a stim (quaff), or flee. Dying is not permanent: you lose 10% of your XP, wake in Ashfall, and an NPC taunts you in front of everyone.

6

Loot, sell, upgrade

get all picks up drops. Gathered materials and looted gear flow into your Bag of Holding automatically. At any shop sell all turns loot into credits (the Salvage Exchange pays most for materials). Spend credits on better gear, potions and cyberware.

7

Level up and unlock

Each level raises your health and mana, and your class unlocks new spells at levels 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 22, 30 and 40. Check the experience table to see what each level costs. New areas open up as you grow: see Areas.

8

Learn a tradeskill

Anyone can learn tradeskills for free at any time: type tradeskills, then learn mining. Your bound tools (fishing pole, mining pick and more) are already yours and improve with use.

9

Reach level 40 and remort

At level 40, go to the Hall of Rebirth and remort into a Dragon, Android, Shapeshifter, Gambler, Ripperdoc, Outlaw or Pirate with stronger skills. You can do it up to 40 times. Read the remort guide.

Ten tips for new runners

  • drink fountain in Ashfall Square restores all your health and mana, free. Come back to it whenever you are hurt.
  • recall teleports you to the starting square any time you are not fighting.
  • Camps (marked C on the map) and the inn regenerate you faster. Safe rooms never have combat.
  • Always carry two healing stims.
  • A look at a monster shows its level and condition.
  • Luck saves you from one killing blow: it halves the damage automatically.
  • Prospectors are gatherers, not fighters. Stick to safe gathering spots.
  • Chrome costs Aura, and low Aura shrinks your mana pool.
  • score shows XP to next level, vitals, wealth and materials.
  • The gossip channel (/g hello) reaches everyone online.
  • Bosses are tough. Bring stims, mana cells and friends.
