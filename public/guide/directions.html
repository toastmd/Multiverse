Directions

Routes from Ashfall Square to every area and its shops, camps and bosses, with ready-to-paste speedwalk commands.

Every route starts in Ashfall Square (where you begin, and where recall takes you). Directions are in words, and as a speedwalk command you can paste into the game: it walks the whole route one step at a time (3n means three steps north). It stops if you cannot go that way or a fight starts, and typing anything else cancels it. Monsters live along many routes, so check the level range first. For town landmarks, travel <place> walks safe rooms only.
