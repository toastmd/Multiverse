Magic and Skills

Spells

Every ability of every class. Spell power grows with your level, so base numbers below are before level scaling.

Cast by typing the spell name (fireball) or cast fireball goblin. Spells cost mana and have a short cooldown (3 seconds unless noted). Damage and healing scale +12% per level past 10, ×1.4 for remort classes, and +3% per remort. Targets resist spell damage with a dice pool (up to 25% off).
