Games

Two card-and-dice games to play in the saloons: Blackjack and Rolling Bones.

Where to play

Both games are played at the saloons: the Silver Spur Saloon in Ashfall, the Rattlesnake Saloon, the Lucky Strike, the Silver Dollar and the Brimstone Saloon Car. Look at the green-felt table in the room (look table) for a quick reminder of the rules. You cannot play while you are fighting.

Blackjack

A hand of cards against the dealer. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

StepWhat happens
1. Sit downType blackjack. The dealer takes 1,000 credits and deals you two cards. The dealer gets two as well, but one stays face down.
2. Count your cardsNumber cards count their number. Jack, Queen and King are 10. An Ace is 11, or 1 if 11 would put you over 21.
3. Hit or standhit draws another card. stand keeps what you have. If you go over 21 you bust and lose your stake. At exactly 21 you stand automatically.
4. The dealer playsThe dealer reveals the hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or more, then stands.
5. Pay-outCloser to 21 than the dealer (or the dealer busts): you win 1,000 on top of your stake. A natural 21 on the deal (Ace and a ten-card) pays 3 to 2. A tie is a push: your 1,000 comes back. Otherwise you lose the 1,000.

Good to know: walking out of the saloon in the middle of a hand forfeits your stake. blackjack rules repeats the rules in the game. Over time the house keeps about 6 credits of every 100 staked, so play for fun.

Rolling Bones (dice)

A dice game for one. You call how two dice will land, bet from 10 to 1,000 credits, and the bones decide. Type bones <bet> <call>, for example bones 100 high (dice works too, and bones alone lists the calls).

CallYou win if...Pays
lowthe total is 2 to 61.25 to 1
highthe total is 8 to 121.25 to 1
seventhe total is exactly 74.5 to 1
doublesboth dice show the same number4.5 to 1
snakesnake eyes: a 1 and a 130 to 1
boxcarsboxcars: a 6 and a 630 to 1

A win returns your bet plus the winnings; a miss costs the bet. Low and high both lose on a seven, which is the house's edge. The long shots (snake eyes and boxcars) pay the most, and they come up about once in 36 rolls.
