Getting Started

Interface and Controls

The browser client has three columns. Here is what everything does.

The three columns

ColumnWhat is in it
LeftThe gossip channel along the bottom, and above it your vitals (a big HP bar, XP and mana), the Quests button, weapon, armor and credits, Luck and Aura, the spellbook with clickable spells, the compass pad, and quick buttons (pack, score, flee, who).
CentreThe game text, the gossip channel pane, and the command line. Up and Down arrows recall earlier commands.
RightThe map, the combat view, and the portrait window.

The map

The map shows every room you have explored, plus all of Ashfall from your first login. Rooms are drawn with their terrain: grass blades, dirt, stone slabs, concrete and riveted metal. Your room has a glowing frame, a YOU label and your class icon. A cyan dot marks another player, a red number counts monsters in your room, and ▲ ▼ mark stairs. Terrain legend sits under the map. See the full world map.

Combat view and portrait

When a fight starts you see both fighters, health bars, flying damage numbers, and a projectile for each spell. Under it, the portrait window shows the NPC you are talking to, or a generated portrait of you (class and gender) when there is no picture.

Quest window

The Quests button (or the Q key) opens a draggable window with only your accepted quests: who gave each, the quest text, every requirement with a progress bar, a READY TO TURN IN badge and where to hand it in, and the reward. It opens by itself the first time you accept a quest and remembers its position.

Settings (the gear button)

Choose the text font (serif, sans, monospace, typewriter, rounded, wide), the size, and turn on keypad controls, which you can re-map to any commands you like. A sound toggle sits under your vitals: level-up, contract and death jingles are original, public-domain (CC0) sounds.

Keypad controls

Turn them on in settings, make sure Num Lock is on, and use these keys whenever the command line is empty:

These are the defaults. In settings, open Edit keypad keys and type a command for any key, for example look;score (separate several commands with ;). You can also type keypad 5 look, keypad reset, or keypad on|off. Your layout is saved to your character, so it follows you to any browser.
