Weapons and Armor

Seven tiers of gear, each with a level requirement. Buy it, loot it, or forge it.

Every weapon and armor piece has a level requirement. Armor slots are body, head, shield and boots (plus a neck slot for charms). Class starting weapons and a free rack of starter gear at Brann's Smithy get you going.

Legendary boss items

Every boss carries one unique legendary item with a 25% drop chance. Equipment versions carry bonus stats far above their level, and treasures are simply priceless. Legendary items sell for their full value (normal gear sells for 40%), and the finder is announced to the whole MUD.
