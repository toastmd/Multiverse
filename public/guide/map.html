All 171 rooms of the world. Hover a room for its name, terrain and features. Use the zoom buttons or scroll.

The surface holds Ashfall and most areas; the underground holds the crypt, the mines, the pharaoh's tomb and the Abyss. Rooms are drawn with their terrain, and paths take the terrain of the room they leave. In the game, Ashfall is mapped from the start and other areas fill in as you explore.
