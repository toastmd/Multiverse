Attributes and Combat

MULTIVERSE MUD fights with pools of six-sided dice. Here is how attacks, defense, Luck and Aura work.

Dice pools

Every attack and defense is a roll of several six-sided dice. A 5 or 6 is a hit. An attack lands when the attacker's hits beat the defender's hits; the surplus ("net hits") adds 6% damage each (up to 8). Six or more net hits is a critical (×1.4). If more than half the dice show 1 and there are no hits, the attack is a fumble.

Spells do not roll to hit. Instead the target resists with a dice pool, shaving up to 25% off the damage.

Health and mana

Health = (30 + level × class health + Grit × 2 + chrome) × (1 + 4% per remort). Mana = (15 + level × class mana + chrome + Mind × 4) × Aura ÷ 6 × (1 + 4% per remort). Spell power grows 12% per level past 10, ×1.4 for remort classes, +3% per remort.

Luck

You hold 3 Luck points (+1 per 10 levels, +1 per 2 Will from implants). A point is spent automatically to halve a blow that would kill you, or on purpose with push for +6 attack dice for two rounds. Luck refills on level-up, with pray and rest, and slowly over time.

Aura

Aura is your integrity, 6.0 at most. Every piece of chrome costs some. Your mana pool is scaled by Aura ÷ 6, so heavy chrome starves casters.

Death

You lose 10% of your XP and wake in Ashfall at half health. A random NPC taunts you by name to everyone online. Nothing is dropped.
