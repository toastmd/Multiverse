Day, Night, Reputation and More

The day and night cycle, light sources, scars, reputation, bodyguards and the World Map.

Day and night

A game day lasts 48 real minutes; the clock sits at the top right. It gets a little darker as the day goes on: brightest at 1 pm, dusk from about 5 pm and night from 9 pm to 5 am (the screen darkens too). At night, outdoors, you need a light: spellcasters (Magician, Cleric, Druid, Alchemist, Necromancer, Bard, Wirewalker, Engineer, Dragon) cast light for mana (15 minutes), and everyone can make torch (20 minutes). In the dark you cannot see the room (only its exits) and you fight at -30%. Towns, buildings, safe rooms and camps are lit.

Scars

Every death leaves a new scar on a random part of your body: a bite from a wolf, a burn from a demon, a bullet from an outlaw. Others see them when they look at you and in finger; scars lists yours.

Reputation

Every quest you complete earns reputation in the area where its quest giver lives (1 point, 3 for a boss quest, +1 for level 100+). It pays +1% credits from kills per point (max +50%), +1% when you sell (max +50%) and 0.5% off in shops (max 30%). The reputation of the area you stand in counts in full, elsewhere a quarter. See reputation, score and the leaderboard. Titles: Stranger, Known, Respected, Trusted, Honored, Revered, Legendary, Mythic.

Bodyguards

The Ironside Bodyguard Agency in Ashfall rents a bodyguard for an hour: hire bodyguard (600 credits per level). He is half your level and strikes your enemy every round, but you earn 10% less experience and credits while he is on the job. dismiss bodyguard sends him away.

The World Map

worldmap, or the World Map button at the top right, shows every room of the world on one page, with your position. The World Map page of this guide shows it too.
