Getting Started

Commands

Everything you can type, grouped by what it does. Type help in the game for a short list.

Movement

CommandWhat it does
n s e w u d ne nw se sw in outWalk (or type the full word, e.g. north or northeast). out also leaves a room that has only one exit; the compass keys have NW, NE, SW, SE and out
go <dir>Walk by name
runto <room id>Run to a room you have explored in this area. Easier: click a room on the map (hover to see its name)
speedwalk n,n,e,e,dWalk a whole route in one command: directions n s e w u d, counts like 3n, up to 60 steps. It stops if you cannot go that way or a fight starts; typing anything else cancels it (speedwalk stop). The Directions page has ready-made routes to every area
recallReturn to the starting square (not while fighting)
fleeTry to run from combat (about 65% success)

Looking

CommandWhat it does
look [thing]See the room, a monster or an item
score (sc)A graphical character sheet in a window: portrait, XP bar, health, mana, aura and luck, an attribute radar chart, attack and defense dice, equipment, wealth, quest progress and tradeskills. score text prints the text version
check <item>Shows a weapon's weapon class (damage range and average) or an armor's armor class, the slot, the level you need and how it compares with what you wear. Anything else answers "This isn't a piece of equipment."
help <command>Detailed help for any command, with usage, examples and related commands (aliases work: help eq). help <ability> explains a class ability
inventory (inv, i, eq)Shows every equipment slot (weapon, head, body, shield, feet) and what is worn in it, then your pack; kept items are marked [kept]
finger <name>A player's name, level, class, picture and email (if they set one). You can also click a name in the Who window
email <address>Add your email to your profile (also in settings). It is shown by finger unless you type email hide; email clear removes it
brief on|offWith brief on, moving through rooms leaves out only the long room description: you still see the room name, exits, items, resources and who is here. look shows the long description too
idea <text>Sends a suggestion to the developer. It is filed in the ideas folder of the GitHub repository as a markdown file, and an issue is opened and assigned to toastmd. 8 to 1500 characters, one every 30 seconds
bug <text>Reports a bug the same way into the bugs folder (with your character, your room and the last lines of text you saw), assigned to toastmd. Say what you were doing and what you expected
commands [category]The list of commands you can use, grouped by category (add a category such as social for descriptions, or a word to search). It also lists your class abilities
whoEveryone online, one per line: name (with any special pre-title), title, class, level and gender. Also opens the portrait window
title <text>Set your own title (up to 40 characters); title clear removes it
mute [bounties|levels|quests|weather] [on|off]Hide bounty, player level-up or quest completion announcements, or weather events (also in settings). mute weather on hides them, mute weather off shows them again
deaths [n | player | top]The death tally: recent player deaths with date, time, player and the NPC that killed them, plus the deadliest NPCs (it is saved between restarts)
weatherWhat the weather is like in your area
blackjack, hit, standPlay blackjack at any saloon for 1000 credits a game: a win pays 1:1, blackjack pays 3:2, a tie returns your stake, and the dealer stands on 17
equipment / inventoryWhat you wear and carry (plus tools and bag)
classYour class summary

Combat and magic

CommandWhat it does
kill <monster>Attack (also: attack, k). With autokill on, a bare kill attacks the first monster in the room
autokill on|offToggle: when on, typing just kill (or k) attacks the first monster in the room. Saved with your character
spellsList your abilities with level and mana cost
cast <spell> [target]Cast a spell (or just type the spell name)
pushSpend Luck for +6 attack dice on your next two rounds
luckSee your Luck points

Items and shopping

CommandWhat it does
get <item|all>Pick things up (loot goes to the bag)
drop <item>Drop an item (bound tools and the bag cannot be dropped)
wield / wear / removeEquip weapons and armor (level requirements apply)
smoke [item]Light a cigarillo to heal about 380 hit points. Old West bosses drop them
upgrade [weapon] [confirm]At a forge (Brann's Smithy) upgrade a weapon from +1 up to +10 for a lot of credits; each upgrade costs 1.75 times the last
enter <thing>Step into a portal or doorway, such as the mysterious portal at the Mirage Gate in the dunes
quaff / eat [item]Use a stim, cell or meal. With no item (eat, or the heal button) it picks the healing item that fits best, from your pack or bag
list / buy [n] <item>Shop (buy several with a number)
sell <item|all|materials>Sell to a shop (the bag too)
keep <item>Mark an item so it is never sold, rsold, salvaged or auctioned (worn items are already protected). keep alone lists them
unkeep <item|all>Make a kept item sellable and salvageable again
value <item>What a shop would pay
prayHeal at the Dawn Clinic
restHeal fully at the inn

Bag of Holding and tools

CommandWhat it does
bagList your bag
bag put <item|all|materials|gear>Store items
bag get <item> [n|all]Take items out
bag sell [materials]Sell bag contents at a shop
bag auto loot|overflow|offChoose when things go into the bag
toolsYour bound tools, their Mark and bonuses

Tradeskills

CommandWhat it does
tradeskills [name]List all tradeskills, or details and recipes
learn <skill>Learn one (free, any time)
forage / mine / fishGather herbs, ore or fish where the room has them
smith / brew / cook <recipe>Craft (smithing needs a forge, cooking a hearth)
salvage <item|all>Break an unworn weapon or armor into materials. salvage all breaks down everything in your pack and bag that is not worn, not marked keep and not legendary

Quests

CommandWhat it does
boardList contracts you can take
talk <npc>Speak to an NPC in the room. They greet you and hand in any finished contract, and a pop-up window shows everything they offer, with an Accept button on each quest and an Accept all button, so you never have to type accept
accept <quest>Accept a contract
questsYour accepted contracts and progress
abandon <quest>Drop a contract

Social

CommandWhat it does
say <text>Talk to the room (also: ' text)
tell <player> <text>Private message
gossip <text>Global channel (also /g in the browser); gossip on|off|history. Add ;word for a GIPHY GIF: gossip ;hi or gossip good game ;applause. Paste a YouTube link to share a playable video with everyone
shout <text>Shout to everyone online
leaderboardThe top 10 achievements: level, quests completed, tradeskills raised and bounty points (also the L key and the top 10 button)
notify on|offHear (or mute) other players logging in and out

Character

CommandWhat it does
autoflee on|offAutomatically try to flee when your health drops to 30% or below
alias <name> <commands>Make your own shortcut, e.g. alias g get all. Separate several commands with ;. Extra words are added to the last command. alias lists them
unalias <name>Remove an alias
keypad [key command | on | off | reset]See or change what the numeric keypad does
notify on|offHear (or mute) login and logout notices
rsell [list]Remote sell: sells every unworn, non-legendary weapon and armor in your pack and bag from anywhere, minus a 10% finder's fee. rsell list previews the total
auction <item> [opening bid]Put equipment or treasure up for auction (anywhere). Bids go up in steps of 100 and nobody can bid more than their credits
bid [amount]Bid on the current auction (just bid raises by 100)
auctionsShow the running auction and the queue (auction off mutes it, auction cancel withdraws yours before any bid)
rob <person>, unlock vault, loot vaultBank jobs in Dry Gulch: hold up stationary people, take the vault key from the Bank Manager, then open and loot the vault
bountiesThe wanted list: quest givers mark monsters as WANTED. Kill one for instant credits and bounty points, which count toward the leaderboard
team [invite|accept|leave|kick|lead]Form a team (up to 5). Kills split XP and credits among teammates in the same room, with +10% per extra member
tc <message>Team chat
socialsList the feelings you can use, such as smile, cheer, dance, wave, bow, laugh, salute, hug (aim one at someone: smile bob)
gag on|offHide (or show) every miss and fumble in combat
trigger add <text> => <command>Run a command automatically when matching text appears (also in settings). triggers lists them
travel <place>Walk to a landmark along safe rooms: guild, smithy, market, armory, temple, saloon, clinic, gate and forest (safe routes only)
tutorial [off|reset]Open (or turn off) the newbie tutorial with clickable steps
gender [male|female|nonbinary]View or change your gender
remortAt level 40 in the Hall of Rebirth
list / install / uninstall / cyberCyberware at the Chrome Clinic and Neural Implant Lab
save / quitSave and leave
