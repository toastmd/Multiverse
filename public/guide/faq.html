FAQ

Quick answers to the questions new players ask most.

Can I change my class?

Only by remorting at level 40, into Dragon, Android or Shapeshifter.

What happens when I die?

You lose 10% of your XP, wake in Ashfall at half health, and a random NPC taunts you to the whole MUD. Nothing else is lost.

How do I heal for free?

Type pray at the Dawn Clinic (free up to level 15), or rest at the inn. Camps and the inn also regenerate you faster.

Why is my mana so low?

Chrome costs Aura, and your mana pool is scaled by your Aura out of 6. Uninstall a piece to get mana back. See Cyberware.

Where do I sell materials?

Any shop buys them. The Salvage Exchange, east of the Ironbound Armory, pays 60% instead of 40%.

What is Luck?

A small pool of points. One automatically halves a blow that would kill you, or push adds six attack dice. It refills on level-up and at the clinics.

Can I drop or sell my bag or tools?

No. They are bound to you, load automatically, and are saved with your character. See Tools and Bag.

How does remort change levelling?

Each remort makes levelling 10% slower but gives +4% health and mana, +3% spell power, a bigger bag and a stronger class.

How do I get around fast?

recall returns you to Ashfall. The map shows exactly where you are.

Do I need to learn tradeskills to gather?

Yes for foraging, mining and fishing (free: learn mining). Prospectors also gather with their class spells.

Why can't I wear my gear after remorting?

You are level 1 again, and gear has level requirements. It waits in your pack.

How do I mute login and logout messages?

Type notify off. Everyone online normally sees a notice when a player logs in or out.

Is gossip shared with Discord?

If the server owner has set up the Discord bridge, gossip you type appears in the server's Discord channel, and messages from that channel appear in gossip as [Discord] name: text.

What are bounties?

Every so often a quest giver marks a monster as WANTED and announces it to everyone. Type bounties to see where. The first player or team to kill it gets credits immediately plus bounty points, which add to the leaderboard score (x40 each).

Can I change how the game looks?

Yes. Open settings (the gear) and pick a theme: black, white, dark grey, dark blue, Old West, futuristic or fantasy. The volume slider is there too.

What is the tutorial?

New characters get a short dialog tour with clickable buttons for every first step. Click Exit tutorial to leave it, or type tutorial to reopen it.

How do teams work?

Type team invite <player>; they type team accept. Credits and XP from each kill are split among teammates in the same room, with a 10% bonus for every extra member.

Do the announcements go to Twitter?

If the server owner has set it up, legendary finds, boss kills, remorts and every 10th level are posted with #game and #Multiverse.

Who are Zeph and Rook?

Resident bot players. They hunt, shop, chat and level like anyone else.

How do I add my own areas?

The server owner can use the grid area builder at /builder.html.

Is the sound free to use?

The three jingles are original and dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
