Cyberware and Implants

Chrome makes you stronger and costs Aura. Neural implants are a rare, expensive series of small stat boosts.

Two clinics sell augmentations. Each piece costs Aura (you have 6.0). Less Aura means a smaller mana pool, so chrome is a trade-off, especially for casters.

Neural Implant Lab: the implant series

West of the clinic (west, west, west). Six attributes, five Marks each. Install in order: Mk I first, then Mk II, and so on. Each upgrade trades the old Mark in for half its price. They are expensive and boost stats a little.

What each attribute does

Brawn adds damage, Reflex adds attack and defense dice, Grit adds health, Mind adds 4 mana per point, Will adds Luck (1 per 2 points), and Charm adds 2% loot credits per point.
