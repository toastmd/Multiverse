Homesteading

Claim land beside the farm, chop trees, mix concrete and build a hut, cabin, house, keep, compound, castle or fortress with rooms of your own.

East of Sunny Acres Farm is the Homestead: a Land Office, a Lumber and Supply Yard, a Well and Sawmill and 51 numbered plots. Stand on a plot and type claim land to own the whole room (first plot 10,000 credits, second 50,000, third 250,000).

The build chain

  1. Chop trees with chop (it teaches you Woodcutting): three or more logs per chop. Planting saplings (plant sapling) gives your plot more trees.
  2. Saw logs into planks and beams: saw plank, saw beam.
  3. Buy supplies at the Supply Yard: thatch, rope, nails, bricks, stone blocks, concrete mix, sand, buckets, glass, iron bars, roof tiles, paint, locks, banners and saplings.
  4. Concrete: fill bucket at the well, mix (mix + sand + water makes wet concrete), form (one wooden form, reusable) and pour: each load cures into a concrete slab.
  5. Build: build hut, then cabin, house, keep, compound, castle and fortress, in that order. Each needs more materials, credits and waiting time. You can log out while it builds.

Inside your home

in and out go through the door. build room <direction> <name> adds rooms (up to the dwelling's limit), roomname and roomdesc describe the room you stand in, and furnish places beds, tables, fireplaces, thrones, trophy cases, fountains and more. home shows your plan and what you are missing; home access open|private and home invite <name> decide who may enter; homes lists your neighbors.
