Remorting

At level 40 you can be reborn at level 1 as a stronger class, up to Remort 40.

Remorting is MULTIVERSE MUD's endgame loop. You reset to level 1, keep the things that cost real effort, and gain permanent bonuses and a remort class with more powerful skills.

How to remort

  1. Reach level 40.
  2. Walk to the Hall of Rebirth: from the starting square, north, west, west.
  3. Type remort to read the details, remort dragon (or android, shapeshifter) to preview, then remort confirm dragon.

Bonuses per remort

Each remort gives +4% maximum health and mana, +3% spell power, +1 attack and defense die every 4 remorts, and +20 Bag of Holding slots. Levelling gets 10% slower each time, to keep progress meaningful.
