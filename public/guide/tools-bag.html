Tools and Bag of Holding

Two things every character has from the start: bound tools that improve with use, and a huge bag that grows with every remort.

Both are autoloading (you already have them), unsellable, non-droppable, and saved to your character when you log out.

One tool per tradeskill. Each improves from Mk I to Mk V by use. Type tools to see yours.

Bag of Holding

A bottomless-feeling bag for loot and materials. Base capacity 60 items, plus 20 for every remort, so Remort 40 holds 860. It stores gathered materials and looted gear automatically, so your pack stays free for potions and equipment.

CommandWhat it does
bagList the contents and capacity
bag put <item|all|materials|gear>Move items from your pack into the bag
bag get <item> [n|all]Take items out
bag sell [materials]Sell the bag's contents at a shop
bag auto lootGathered materials and looted gear go straight into the bag (default)
bag auto overflowOnly use the bag when your pack is full
bag auto offNever use the bag

Crafting and salvage use materials and gear from the bag. sell all and sell materials sell bag contents too. Drops, quest rewards and purchases go to your pack (and overflow to the bag).
