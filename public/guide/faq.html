FAQ

Quick answers to the questions new players ask most.

Can I change my class?

Only by remorting at level 40, into Dragon, Android, Shapeshifter, Gambler, Ripperdoc, Outlaw or Pirate.

What happens when I die?

You lose 10% of your XP, wake in Ashfall at half health, and a random NPC taunts you to the whole MUD. Nothing else is lost.

How do I heal for free?

Type pray at the Dawn Clinic (free up to level 15), or rest at the inn. Camps and the inn also regenerate you faster.

Why is my mana so low?

Chrome costs Aura, and your mana pool is scaled by your Aura out of 6. Uninstall a piece to get mana back. See Cyberware.

Where do I sell materials?

Any shop buys them. The Salvage Exchange, east of the Ironbound Armory, pays 60% instead of 40%.

What is Luck?

A small pool of points. One automatically halves a blow that would kill you, or push adds six attack dice. It refills on level-up and at the clinics.

Can I drop or sell my bag or tools?

No. They are bound to you, load automatically, and are saved with your character. See Tools and Bag.

How does remort change levelling?

Each remort makes levelling 10% slower but gives +4% health and mana, +3% spell power, a bigger bag and a stronger class.

How do I get around fast?

recall returns you to Ashfall. The map shows exactly where you are.

Do I need to learn tradeskills to gather?

Yes for foraging, mining and fishing (free: learn mining). Prospectors also gather with their class spells.

Why can't I wear my gear after remorting?

You are level 1 again, and gear has level requirements. It waits in your pack.

How do I mute login and logout messages?

Type notify off. Everyone online normally sees a notice when a player logs in or out.

Is gossip shared with Discord?

If the server owner has set up the Discord bridge, gossip you type appears in the server's Discord channel, and messages from that channel appear in gossip as [Discord] name: text.

How does the auction work?

Anyone can `auction` an unworn weapon, armor or treasure. Auctioneer Quincy Pike announces it and every bid. When bidding goes quiet he calls going once, waits 5 seconds, going twice, waits 5 more, then SOLD: the winner pays and receives the item, and the seller receives the credits. Any new bid restarts the count.

How do I rob the bank?

Take the Bank Robbery contract from Slick Sam in the Rattlesnake Saloon, then walk east into the bank. rob teller holds up stationary people (guards come running if you fail). Kill Bank Manager Ebenezer Crane, pick up his brass key and use unlock vault, then loot vault. The vault holds a different sum each time. You can repeat the contract, but quest points are awarded only once.

What is the Welcome to the Multiverse quest?

A quest you get automatically. Finish the newbie tutorial (press Finish on its last step) to earn quest points, credits, two stims and XP. It is rewarded once.

Is there weather?

Yes, by area (with sounds: rain, thunder and a tornado roar). Old West areas get rain, dust devils and the occasional tornado. The Modern district gets rain and snow. Sci-fi areas get electrical storms and electromagnetic pulses. You see it as messages and as an animation over the room picture; indoors you only hear it muffled (an EMP reaches everywhere). It never hurts you, and mute weather on hides it.

Who is killing everybody?

Type deaths. Every death is logged with the date, time, player and the NPC that killed them, and deaths top ranks the deadliest NPCs.

How do I stop something being sold or salvaged?

Type keep <item>. Kept items show as [kept] in inventory and are skipped by sell, sell all, rsell, salvage, salvage all and the auction. unkeep <item> (or unkeep all) undoes it.

How do I see someone's picture or email?

Type finger <name> or click their name in the Who window. Add your own email with email you@example.com or in settings; email hide keeps it private.

Can I turn off long room descriptions?

Yes: brief on leaves out only the long description as you move (the room name, exits, items, resources and NPCs still show), brief off brings it back, and look always shows the full room.

How do I accept a quest without typing?

Talk to the NPC (talk torv) or read the board: a pop-up lists the quests with an Accept button for each. A team invitation also opens a pop-up with Accept and Decline buttons.

How do I send an idea or report a bug?

Type idea <your idea> or bug <what happened>. They go straight to the developer's GitHub as files in the ideas and bugs folders, with an issue assigned to toastmd.

Where do Zeph and Rook go?

They train in the Proving Grounds, a fenced field behind the little gate in the Ashfall back alley (type in there). They stay there so the rest of the world is yours; visitors are welcome.

How do I get to the dinosaurs?

Go to the Mirage Gate in the Sunscorch Dunes and enter portal. The Primeval Rift (levels 21 to 28) has raptors, pterosaurs, triceratops and Rex the Tyrant. Dr. Mara Quill's camp at the landing has quests and a shop; a portal there takes you home.

Where are the vampires and demons?

Nightfall Hollow (levels 33 to 39): from the Shattered Citadel's Crypt Gate type in. Father Aldric's chapel is the safe place; vampires hold the manor to the north, werewolves the woods, witches the crossroads and demons the Brimstone Gate at the east end.

How do I cast with a long name?

You do not have to: every ability is a one-word command with no underscores. Type the word (rage, fireball, song), with a target if needed. spells lists the words.

What is the taunts channel?

When a player dies an NPC may taunt them, on the taunts channel (green). Every death is also announced on the deaths channel. Both can be switched off with mute taunts on and mute deaths on. Bots are never taunted.

Where are the dinosaurs, the caveman and the CEO?

Dinosaurs: the Primeval Rift (enter the portal at the Mirage Gate). Caveman: the Mammoth Caverns, in from Stegosaur Valley in the Rift (Grog the Great waits in the deepest cavern). The CEO: Meridian Tower, north of Commerce Avenue in the Modern district, floor 10 (elevator top).

How do I travel instantly?

Find the milestone (a standing stone, listed in the room) in each area, touch milestone to attune, and from any milestone use milestone <area> (or the Travel buttons in the window) to jump to any milestone you have touched. milestones shows where they all are.

Can I get my mudmail by email?

Yes: put your email on your profile, mail forward on, and confirm the code you are sent. Mudmail then arrives in your inbox too, whether or not you are in the game.

How do I become an Architect?

Type help builder: it explains the ranks (Player, Architect, Administrator), what you can build (any area is open for discussion), and how to apply. Architects use a point-and-click grid builder.

What are bounties?

Every 15 minutes a quest giver marks a monster as WANTED and announces it to everyone. Type bounties to see where. The first player or team to kill it gets credits immediately plus bounty points, which add to the leaderboard score (x40 each).

Can I change how the game looks?

Yes. Open settings (the gear) and pick a theme: black, white, dark grey, dark blue, Old West, futuristic or fantasy. The volume slider is there too.

How do I get somewhere quickly?

Open the Directions page, copy the speedwalk line for your destination (for example speedwalk 5e,s,u) and paste it into the game. For town landmarks you can also type travel guild, travel smithy and so on.

What can I type?

Type commands (or press the commands button) for every command by category and your class abilities.

How do I get to the Modern district?

In Ashfall Square, type up: you step through the shimmering arch onto a modern strip with the Multi-Mart superstore, a shopping center, a trailer park, burgers, hot dogs, soda and gas stations with restrooms. The Directions page has routes to every shop and department.

What is the tutorial?

New characters get a short dialog tour with clickable buttons for every first step. Click Exit tutorial to leave it, or type tutorial to reopen it.

How do teams work?

Type team invite <player>; they type team accept. Credits and XP from each kill are split among teammates in the same room, with a 10% bonus for every extra member.

Do the announcements go to Twitter?

If the server owner has set it up, legendary finds, boss kills, remorts and every 10th level are posted with #game and #Multiverse.

Who are Zeph and Rook?

Resident bot players. They hunt, shop, chat and level like anyone else.

How do I add my own areas?

The server owner can use the grid area builder at /builder.html.

Is the sound free to use?

The three jingles are original and dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
