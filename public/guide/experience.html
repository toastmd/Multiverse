Experience and Levels

How much XP each level takes, what unlocks when, and how remorting changes it.

You gain XP from monsters and contracts. To leave level L you need a total of 50 × L² experience, so each level costs a little more than the last. At level 40 you stop levelling and can remort. score tells you exactly how many XP you still need.

Remort changes the price

The totals above are multiplied by 1 + 0.1 × your remort number. At Remort 5 that is ×1.5, at Remort 10 ×2, at Remort 40 ×5. See the remort table for the full list.
