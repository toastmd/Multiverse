Classes

Twelve classes to start, three more through remorting. Each has its own spells, health and mana, and playstyle.

Class stat modifiers are added to your base attributes. Health/level and mana/level are what you gain per level. Regen is bonus mana every two seconds. The three portraits on each card show female, male and nonbinary versions of your generated character portrait.
